PHEBUS backup

Saves everything on the FM-1 in one file: the music you are working on, the sections A to D, the user presets, the MY HEX kits and the settings. Put it back later, or after installing another firmware and coming back. Chrome or Edge, the FM-1 on USB, stopped.

A restore replaces what is on the FM-1. Older backups (KARMA, SLOOP) restore too; their sample slots are skipped (PHEBUS has none).
