PHEBUS cheat sheet

Ben's FM-1 firmware, a fork of KARMA: MiniFreak-style synth voices, Rytm-style drums, MPC-style performance pads. One idea per control: EDIT the sound, ENV the envelopes, LFO the modulation, FX the effects, SEQ the rhythm and the patterns, ARP the notes, GLO everything global, SAVE keeps and confirms, REC only records, OCT- / OCT+ minus and plus. Four ways to move, in order: a labelled button opens its first category (again: the next category); SEL is PAGE, the next page inside it; SELECT is knob 5, the TYPE (on the drum screen: the sound); holds are shortcuts or performance layers. Hold a button and turn MASTER: that button's broad parameter. Unreleased: not yet run on a real FM-1. Do not flash until the unit's chip revision is checked.

Colour = the voice it acts on:1 purple2 blue3 coraldrums tealshared (tag GLO, MIX, PERFORM): white / grey. Yellow M = mute, ice-blue S = solo, red = record / erase only

Getting around

ALGORITHMVoice: 1, 2, 3, DRUM, on every screen, nothing more (the STUTTER lane: FX's STUT)
PRESETSThe voice's sounds. Drums: on the drum screen the selected slot's SOUND; on HOME the whole KIT (asks only over a kit you changed)
OCT- / OCT+Tap: the screen's minus / plus (steps page, pattern, step, sound, preset, or the knob last touched one fine step). Twice within 0.3 s: octave -3 to +3 (both: 0)
- + legendAt the tab row's right: [-] WORD [+], what a tap does. None: a tap does nothing there
tab rowThe categories of the button you pressed (the one shown filled) and the page inside ("2/4")
SAVE + OCT-Undo. SAVE + OCT+: redo
keep openHold any layer button 0.7 s untouched, let go: it stays (LOCK). The same button or any other lets go
confirmHOLD SAVE: ... in a white bar? Hold SAVE until the ring fills (red: something goes; white: it only keeps). Let go early, tap SAVE or press another button: nothing
MASTERNothing held: the volume. A button held: its pair (FX MIX, SWING, TRANSP, LEVEL, ENV LENGTH, MOD DEPTH, TEMPO), named in the header's chip. Relative: nothing jumps

DRUMThe kit's name (CUSTOM once a slot differs from it), the sounds muted or soloed on the mixer, the selected sound, the 16 steps: its hits teal, other sounds' dim.
PERFORMSTUTTER lane ON / OFF, FX>STUT (where it lives) or the latched pads by name, the lane's steps as spans.
CUTOFFK1: the voice filter
DECAYK2: the moving envelope
STUTK3: lane on / off
LEVELK4: the voice

Above the zones: the bar, the FLOW strip (Ben kept it), patterns A B C D (playing filled, next outlined). The header says PATTERN A to D while one plays; a white dot: unsaved. The > after the strip: HOME again opens the screensaver (SEL: the next one).

The steps tap SEQ, any voice

PLACETap an empty step: set with the pen (last note or chord played, or K1), velocity 100. Tap a set step: cleared when the key comes up (unless a knob was turned).
HOLD A STEPTurn any knob: it locks what that knob shows on the step (shown inverted). On TRIG: K1 NOTE, K2 LEN (ties), K3 VEL, K4 COND; TRIG 2/2: FLAG, RETRIG, NUDGE. Several steps edit together.
PAGESOCT- / OCT+ tapped: the page before / after (OCT+ on the last adds one, a copy). Max 64. The view follows the playhead.
buttonsNothing held: EDIT, ENV, LFO, FX open their real pages (the steps go, the keys play notes). A step held: their knobs under the steps, a lock view (LOCK: in the tab row); let go: back to TRIG. SEQ: TRIG, PATTERN, A-D
nothing heldTRIG: K1 NOTE (pen), K2 STEP (cursor), K3 HIT (right sets, left clears), K4 VEL. PRESETS the sound
Spice and DiceTRIG 2/2 (SEL on TRIG), nothing held: K1 SPICE (0: the pattern as written), K2 DICE (each click right a new roll, left the roll before). The tiles show the variation; the notes never change
step + OCT-Minus removes: on TRIG the step itself; on a lock view the lock of the knob last turned, again every lock, again the step. 24 locks a voice

  • SELECT: the sound (1 of 16, heard while stopped; the header's chip names it). EDIT = SRC, again FLTR. ENV = AMP. LFO = LFO. FX = FILTER, INS, SENDS, SLICER, then MIX, STUT. SEQ = TRIG, again PATTERN. SEL: the page inside (SRC 1/3 .., AMP 2/2, TRIG 2/2).
  • The kit is 16 slots, the project's own; each holds any sound from any kit, HEX or CLASSIC. Nothing held: a knob edits the selected slot's sound at once.
  • PRESETS on the drum screen: the selected slot's sound (SOUND 808 SNARE): INIT HEX, INIT CLASSIC, then its kind from every kit, then every other kind. Turn back: the sound you had. SAVE + OCT-: undo.
  • Hold a step + turn: a lock on that sound's hit (shown inverted). OCT-: that lock off; again: every lock; again (or on TRIG): the hit. On TRIG: VEL RETRIG NUDGE COND / CHANCE LENGTH CURVE NOTE.
  • Mutes and solos: hold GLO (the white keys mute the 16 sounds; OCT+ held with a key: solo; OCT-: all off). ARP tapped: CHROMATIC (keys play the sound at pitches; -/+ the sound, twice quickly the octave). SEQ held + key: RETRIG (K1 the rate).
  • FILL: hold SEQ and PLAY (OCT+ latches). A sound's key held + OCT- (where the keys play sounds): its hits erased. PRESETS on HOME loads a whole kit (power-on: 606). FLTR: CUTOFF RESO ENV TYPE (2/2 DRIVE); FX: the drum voice's own FILTER, INSERT, SENDS, SLICER, then MIX. A whole kit over a changed one: HOLD SAVE. Your kits: SAVE > KITS (MY KIT 1-6: KIT, LOAD, ERASE, SAVE).
  • Keys 1-16: KICK, KICK 2, SNARE, CLAP, HAT, OPEN HAT, PEDAL, RIM, SNARE 2, LOW TOM, HI TOM, CRASH, RIDE, SHAKER, CONGA, COWBELL.

STUTTER lane tap FX until STUT: after MIX

  • FX's last category, any voice (it stays selected; ALGORITHM changes it, the lane stays). Tag MIX, title STUTTER, white. FX again: the voice's INS. Hold FX: PERFORM, as anywhere.
  • Tap an unlit step: it lights (new steps 1/4). Tap a lit step: it goes out on release.
  • Hold a step + K1: its length 1/1, 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32. Hold a step + OCT-: erase the recorded pads over it.
  • K1: length for new steps. K2: lane ON / OFF (steps and recorded pads kept; HOME K3 the same). K3 PADS: how many pads are recorded; turn, then hold SAVE to clear them. K4: lane length 1-64.
  • OCT- / OCT+ tapped: page. SAVE + OCT-: undo.
  • A lit step repeats the span just before it, the whole mix (1/2 and 1/1 replay the steps). Same-length steps in a row are one loop, drawn as one span.
  • PERFORM pads played while recording land here as named bars and play back with the pattern (31 at most).
  • REC here: the lane's own take, no voice armed (stopped: PLAY starts it). Hold SEQ, ARP, EDIT: the selected voice's layers (SEQ's halve / double: not here).
  • Black keys: hand-played repeats of the whole mix, F#3 1/1 · G#3 1/2 · A#3 1/4 · C#4 1/8 · D#4 1/16 · F#4 1/4T · G#4 1/8T · A#4 1/16T · C#5 1/8D · D#5 1/16D · F#5 1/32 (OCT+ held: latch; recorded into the lane while recording; a key held wins over the lit steps).

One time effect at a time (the last pressed: a repeat, HALF, REVERSE, STOP, JET), with every colour effect, the throws and the mutes on top. Drum mutes held together: you hear what all of them leave. PERFORM's mutes never touch the mixer's. A muted part plays on unheard and comes back in place. REC while playing records the pads into the STUTTER lane.

Saving

  • White dot in the header: unsaved changes.
  • The working project saves itself after you stop, and comes back at power-on.
  • Patterns A to D: saved when you store them (hold SAVE + key 1-4).
  • User presets: SAVE > USER.

Words

stepA place in a pattern; a white key on the GRID. "STEP 5 HELD"
trigWhat is on a step: a note or a hit. TRIG: the tab with the held step's values
lockA value on one step: hold the step, turn. Drawn inverted
RETRIGA step repeated: a drum hit at a rate, a synth step x1 to x4
PERFORMThe pads layer (hold FX only). Tapped, FX is this voice's effects, then MIX and STUT (the whole mix)
categoryWhat a button opens, one a press (EDIT: OSC, FILT). SEL walks its pages
typeWhat SELECT turns: the Type, the filter's TYPE, an insert's TYPE, the LFO's WAVE, the SLICER's mode
SENDA voice's share of the whole mix's delay, reverb or chorus (its SENDS page)
patternA to D: all four voices, the kit and the lane
