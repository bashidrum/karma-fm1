PHEBUS cheat sheet

Ben's FM-1 firmware, a fork of KARMA. Tap a button to open its pages (tap again, or turn SELECT, for the next). Hold a button for its layer on the keys and knobs. Unreleased: not yet run on a real FM-1.

Colour = the voice it acts on:1 purple2 blue3 tealdrums coralshared (tag ALL): white / grey. Yellow M = mute, ice-blue S = solo, red = record / erase only

The GRID tap SEQ, any voice

PLACETap an empty step: set with the pen (last note or chord played, or K1), velocity 100. Tap a set step: cleared when the key comes up (unless a knob was turned).
HOLD A STEPTurn any knob: it locks what that knob shows on the step (shown inverted). On TRIG: K1 NOTE, K2 LEN (ties), K3 VEL, K4 COND; TRIG 2/2: FLAG, RATCH, NUDGE. Several steps edit together.
PAGESBlack keys 1-4: steps 1-16, 17-32, 33-48, 49-64. The next empty slot adds a page. OCT-/OCT+: page (OCT+ on the last adds one). Max 64. The view follows the playhead.
tabsTRIG OSC FILT ENV FX MOD, as the drum screen's. EDIT: OSC, again the next stage. ENV, FX, LFO: their tab (again: its next half). SEQ: TRIG (again: back). SELECT: every half. With a step held too
nothing heldA sound tab: the knobs edit the sound. TRIG: K1 NOTE (pen), K2 STEP (cursor), K3 HIT (right sets, left clears), K4 VEL. PRESETS the sound
step + OCT- / OCT+OCT-: the lock of the knob last turned off; again: every lock (on TRIG: locks, nudge, condition). OCT+: next condition. 24 locks a voice

Drums the Rytm's way

tap SEQ (or EDIT) with DRUM selected: the drum screen. The 16 keys are the selected sound's steps

  • Pages: EDIT = SRC, again FLTR, AMP, LFO. ENV = AMP, LFO = LFO (again: the page's next half). SEQ = TRIG (again: back). SELECT walks every half.
  • Nothing held: a knob edits the selected sound. The first turn on a built-in kit makes your own copy (kit name gets a *).
  • Hold a step + turn: a lock on that trig (shown inverted). OCT-: that lock off; again: every lock. On TRIG: VEL RETRIG NUDGE COND / CHANCE LENGTH CURVE NOTE.
  • SEL held + key: pick the sound. SEL tapped: MUTE mode (keys mute, SEL + key solos). ARP tapped: CHROMATIC (keys play the sound at pitches, OCT-/OCT+ octave). ARP held + key: RETRIG (black keys pick the rate).
  • F#5 on the drum screen: FILL while held, twice quickly: latched. PRESETS picks the kit (power-on: 606). FX: the drum voice's own INSERT, SLICER, FILTER.
  • Keys 1-16: KICK, KICK 2, SNARE, CLAP, HAT, OPEN HAT, PEDAL, RIM, SNARE 2, LOW TOM, HI TOM, CRASH, RIDE, SHAKER, CONGA, COWBELL.

STUTTER lane ALGORITHM past DRUM

  • Tap an unlit step: it lights (length 1/8). Tap a lit step: it goes out on release.
  • Hold a step + K1: its length 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32.
  • K2: lane ON / OFF (steps kept). K4: lane length 1-64.
  • Black keys 1-4, OCT-/OCT+: page. EDIT + OCT-: undo.
  • A lit step repeats the span just before it, the whole mix. Same-length steps in a row are one loop.

Saving

  • White dot in the header: unsaved changes.
  • The working project saves itself after you stop, and comes back at power-on.
  • Sections, song order and SONGS 1-3: saved when you say so.
  • User presets: SAVE > USER.

Power-on and recovery

OCT-Hold while switching on: USB rescue
OCT- + OCT+Hold while switching on: hardware calibration
OCT- + OCT+ 5 sStopped: update mode (UBOOT). Let go to cancel

Some FM-1 hardware revisions can be bricked by custom firmware. Check your unit before flashing.
